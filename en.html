Mihail Mitov

Executive director of UCO Bulgaria. Manager from Gabrovo, former Member of the 51st National Assembly and former executive director of FC Yantra Gabrovo.

Biography

Mihail Iliev Mitov studied sport management at Solent University in Southampton, England, where he worked for The Football Association and Southampton FC. He holds certificates from The FA and the University of Barcelona, and in 2024 graduated with distinction from the UEFA Certificate in Football Management.

He has been part of FC Yantra Gabrovo since the men's team returned to professional football and has been its executive director since 2022.

On 17 January 2025 he was sworn in as a GERB Member of the 51st National Assembly of Bulgaria for the Gabrovo constituency, replacing Tomislav Donchev, as one of the youngest MPs. His focus is sport, opportunities for young Bulgarians and the development of Gabrovo Province.

He is now executive director of UCO Bulgaria, a Gabrovo-based company that collects used cooking oil and fats from restaurants and hotels across the country for processing into biofuels.
