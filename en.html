Mihail Mitov

CEO of UCO Bulgaria. Circular economy and biofuels, sports management, youth and European policy.

Biography

Mihail Iliev Mitov is CEO of UCO Bulgaria, the pioneer in waste oil recycling in Bulgaria. The company collects used cooking oil from more than 8,000 clients and supplies it as certified feedstock for biodiesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF).

He was previously CEO of FC Yantra Gabrovo (2021–2025), where he grew annual sponsorship revenue by 300%, secured 50 new commercial partners and multi-million-euro investment in sports infrastructure, and built a professional youth academy of 300 children. From 2022 to 2025 he sat on the Board of Directors of M-texx Textile Recycling.

From January 2025 to April 2026 he was a GERB Member of Bulgaria's 51st National Assembly for Gabrovo, serving on the Committee on Youth and Sports and the Committee on European Affairs and European Funds. From June 2025 to April 2026 he was a member of the Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe (Committee on Political Affairs and Democracy; Committee on Equality and Non-Discrimination).

He holds a First-Class Honours degree in Sports Studies and Business from Solent University, Southampton, where he also worked for Southampton FC and the Hampshire FA, and the UEFA Certificate in Football Management (University of Lausanne) with Distinction. He is an alumnus of the U.S. State Department's International Visitor Leadership Program for Young European Leaders and a master's candidate in Youth Policy and Sports at the National Sports Academy “Vassil Levski”.
